Read this first

  • This is a wired flash, not Bluetooth. A modified firmware can't go over Bluetooth — the scooter's radio chip checks a Xiaomi signature and rejects it. Flashing goes over the controller's UART.
  • You need a USB‑to‑TTL adapter (CP2102 or CH340, a few dollars) wired to the controller's UART pads: GND↔GND, adapter TX↔RX, adapter RX↔TX. 3.3V logic.
  • Back up your stock firmware first. Download the exact stock .bin for your scooter from mi‑fw‑info and keep it — flashing it back is your undo.
  • Desktop Chrome or Edge only (Web Serial). A failed flash can leave the controller needing a re‑flash; keep it powered and connected until it finishes.
